Reading a Q-Q plot

Draw a sample from a chosen shape. The histogram shows its shape. The Q-Q plot lines up your sorted data against where a bell curve (normal) would put them: dots on the straight line mean "normal". The Shapiro–Wilk test turns the same idea into a p-value.
