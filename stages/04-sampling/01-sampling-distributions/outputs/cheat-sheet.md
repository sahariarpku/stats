# Cheat sheet: Sampling distribution of x̄

**Sampling distribution** = distribution of a statistic over all possible samples of size n.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Centre | μ (x̄ is unbiased) |
| Spread (standard error) | σ ÷ √n |
| Shape | Bell if the population is bell-shaped, or n large (CLT) |

**Bigger n → narrower.** Halving the spread needs 4× the sample.

**Three different spreads**
- σ: spread of the **population**
- s: spread of **your sample**
- σ/√n: spread of the **sample means**

**Tiny example:** population {2,4,6}, n = 2 → 9 samples, means 2,3,4,3,4,5,4,5,6 → centre 4 = μ, SD 1.1547 = σ/√2.

**Traps**
1. Confusing σ with σ/√n.
2. Thinking you observe the sampling distribution. You only ever see one sample from it.
3. Expecting larger n to move the centre (it only narrows the spread).
