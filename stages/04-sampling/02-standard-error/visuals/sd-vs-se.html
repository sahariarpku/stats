SD stays, SE shrinks

Each dot is one person's score in a random sample. In the whole population the average is 50 and people typically differ by about 10 (σ = 10). The wide band is ±1 SD: how much people vary. The narrow band is ±1 SE: how sure we are of the average. Raise n and press "New sample".
